> TL;DR: Raise the audit price, package it with a monthly retainer, and test the
> new offer on the next three inbound leads before changing the public page.

So I keep thinking about the pricing again. The audit alone is underpriced — every
client who bought it came back for hardening anyway, which means the audit is the
door, not the product. Package: audit plus first month of retainer, one price.
Test it quietly on the next three inbound leads, do not touch the public page yet.
If two of three take it, it becomes the default offer.
